# The Foundation of SaaS Growth: RevOps & Data Hygiene
One of the biggest bottlenecks in SaaS growth isn't lead generation—it's operations. If your marketing and sales teams are working in siloed CRMs with messy data, you're leaking revenue.

### The Fundamentals
Before investing in expensive automation tools, focus on:
1. **Unified data sources**
2. **Clear handover processes**
3. **Clean, standardized tagging**

*Automation scales what already works. If your process is broken, automation just breaks it faster.*
